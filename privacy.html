  • The Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)
  • The Japanese Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI)
  • The South Korean Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA)
  • Other applicable national and regional privacy laws

3. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data whatsoever. The App does not collect, process, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or personal data as defined under any applicable privacy law. This includes but is not limited to:

  • Names, email addresses, postal addresses, or phone numbers
  • Device identifiers, IMEI, serial numbers, or advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA)
  • Precise or approximate location data (GPS, Wi-Fi, cell tower)
  • IP addresses (we operate no servers that could receive them)
  • Usage analytics, session data, or crash reports
  • Browsing history, search history, or app usage patterns
  • Biometric data, health data, or genetic data
  • Financial information, payment details, or wallet addresses
  • Contacts, calendar data, or call logs
  • Photos, videos, or media files
  • Any data classified as "sensitive information" under the Australian Privacy Act
  • Any "special category data" under Article 9 of the GDPR

4. Local Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Ticker preferences: Your selected cryptocurrency tickers (e.g., SOL, SKR) — stored as string values
  • Cached prices: Recently fetched price data (numerical values and timestamps) for widget display

This data:

  • Is stored exclusively on your physical device in the app's private sandboxed storage
  • Is never transmitted to any server operated by us or any third party
  • Is not accessible to other applications on your device
  • Is automatically and permanently deleted when you uninstall the App
  • Can be manually cleared at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Sol Price Widget > Clear Data

5. Network Requests & Third-Party API Providers

The App makes HTTPS requests directly from your device to the following third-party public APIs to retrieve cryptocurrency price data:

  • Jupiter Aggregator API — jup.ag (operated by Jupiter Exchange)
  • Raydium API — api-v3.raydium.io (operated by Raydium Protocol)
  • CoinGecko API — api.coingecko.com (operated by Gecko Labs Pte. Ltd.)

Important: We do not operate any intermediate servers, proxies, or backend infrastructure. All API requests are made directly from your device to the third-party providers listed above. We have no access to, and do not log, any aspect of these communications.

As part of standard HTTPS/TLS communication, your device's IP address is necessarily transmitted to these third-party API providers. This is an inherent aspect of internet communication and is outside our control. We recommend you review the privacy practices of these providers:

  • CoinGecko Privacy Policy
  • Jupiter Documentation
  • Raydium Documentation

6. No Accounts, Registration, or Authentication

The App does not require or support any form of account creation, user registration, login, authentication, or identity verification. There is no user account system.

7. No Analytics, Advertising, or Tracking

The App contains:

  • No analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar)
  • No advertising frameworks (no AdMob, Facebook Ads, Unity Ads, or similar)
  • No tracking pixels, web beacons, or fingerprinting scripts
  • No crash reporting SDKs (no Crashlytics, Sentry, Bugsnag, or similar)
  • No social media SDKs (no Facebook SDK, Twitter SDK, or similar)
  • No attribution or marketing SDKs

8. Permissions

The App requests only the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET — Required to fetch live cryptocurrency prices from the third-party public APIs listed in Section 5
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — Required to restart the automatic price update schedule after your device reboots

The App does not request or use any permissions related to location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, phone state, SMS, or any other sensitive Android permissions.

9. Automated Decision-Making

Sol Price Widget performs one automated decision: sanctions region screening at app startup using SIM card, network carrier, and device locale signals. If your device is detected in a sanctioned jurisdiction, the App displays a compliance screen restricting access. This decision is based on legitimate interest in regulatory compliance (GDPR Art. 22(2)(b)) and does not produce legal effects beyond restricting access to the App’s informational features. You may contact us at aardappvark@proton.me to contest this decision.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 16 (or 13 under COPPA in the United States). We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Since we collect no personal data from any user, no special provisions for children’s data are required. The App displays cryptocurrency market data and is intended for users aged 18 and over.

11. International Data Transfers

Since we collect and store no personal data, there are no international transfers of personal data by us. The direct API requests from your device to third-party providers (Section 5) may involve data transmission to servers located in various countries. These transmissions are initiated by your device and are governed by the respective privacy policies of those providers.

12. Data Security

All network communications between your device and third-party APIs use HTTPS with TLS encryption. Since no personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted by us, data security risks are inherently minimised. Local data stored on your device is protected by Android's application sandboxing architecture.
