3. Price Data Accuracy Disclaimer

Cryptocurrency price data is sourced from third-party APIs (Jupiter Aggregator, Raydium, CoinGecko) and:

  • May be delayed — Prices are fetched at approximately 15-minute intervals and may not reflect the most current market price
  • May be inaccurate — Third-party data sources may provide incorrect data due to API errors, calculation errors, or data feed issues
  • May be incomplete — Not all trading pairs or exchanges are represented; data may be missing for some tokens
  • May be entirely unavailable — API services may experience downtime, rate limiting, or discontinuation
  • May differ from other sources — Prices on different exchanges and data providers vary due to liquidity, time zone, and market microstructure differences
  • Are not audited or verified — We do not independently verify the accuracy of data from our third-party sources

Under no circumstances should you rely on prices displayed by Sol Price Widget for trading, investment, or any financial decisions.
